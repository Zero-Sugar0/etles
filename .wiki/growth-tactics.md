# Growth Tactics — The Playbook

*Acquisition channels, referral mechanics, community-led growth, and launch playbooks. What actually works in 2025–2026 for B2B and B2C.*

---

## Pick a Channel, Then Go Deep

Not every channel fits every business. Choose 1–2 and commit before adding more. Five channels at 20% effort each is a slower way to fail at all of them.

**How to choose:**
1. Where does your ICP already spend time? Fish where the fish are.
2. Where's your unfair advantage — network, content, distribution?
3. What's your CAC tolerance against LTV? Some channels cost more but convert harder.
4. How fast do you need results? Paid is fast, SEO is slow, community sits in between.

| Channel | Time to first result | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| Paid social | Days | High, budget-limited |
| Cold outbound | A week | Medium, effort-limited |
| Content/SEO | 3–6 months | Very high |
| Community | 3–6 months | Medium–high |
| Referral/viral | Variable | Very high |
| Partnerships | 1–3 months | High |
| Product Hunt | One day | Medium, one-time spike |

## Referral Programs That Actually Work

**The five mechanics that make one work:**
1. **Both-sided reward** — giver and receiver both get something (Dropbox: storage for both; Uber: credit for both)
2. **Zero-friction sharing** — pre-written message, unique link, one click
3. **Reward matches the product** — more of your product, not a generic gift card
4. **Trigger at the right moment** — after a first win, never on signup
5. **Visible progress** — show users exactly how close they are

**Don't launch a referral program on a product that isn't yet worth sharing.** A weak product with a referral loop just spreads the bad word faster.

**Share-rate benchmarks:**
- Under 1% → friction problem or wrong trigger moment
- 1–5% → normal for B2B
- 5%+ → the product is genuinely loved and the program is well-built

## Community-Led Growth

Works when people get more value from the connection than from the software alone — SaaS, creator tools, professional development, niche B2B.

**Five stages:**
1. **Seed (0–50):** Hand-pick founding members. Personally invite people already discussing the problem. No automation.
2. **Build (50–200):** Create rituals — a weekly event, a recurring format, a regular "why we're here" post.
3. **Sustain (200–1,000):** Find your 10–15 super-members. Give them recognition, early access, or a role — they do more than you'd expect.
4. **Scale (1,000+):** Split into sub-communities by role, interest, or geography. A 10,000-person community with no structure is a dead one.
5. **Monetize:** Charge for deeper access, premium events, certification, job placement — never the core community itself.

**Platform fit:**
- **Discord** — developer, gaming, creator communities; high engagement, lower signal
- **Slack** — professional B2B; lower ceiling, more serious members
- **Circle/Mighty Networks** — paid communities, course + community combos
- **Reddit** — never try to own a subreddit; contribute to existing ones

## Product Hunt Launch Playbook

**Only one metric matters: #1 product of the day.** Everything else is noise.

**Three weeks out:**
- Pick Tuesday or Wednesday — Monday is slow, Thursday/Friday fade fast
- Find a hunter with 1,000+ followers in your category; DM a specific, personal ask
- Build the assets: one scroll-stopping hero image, 3–5 gallery images, a 60-second demo, a tagline under 60 characters
- Launch a "coming soon" page and start collecting followers early
- Comment on other PH launches so you have history before your own

**Launch day (12:01am PST):**
- Post at exactly 12:01am — the ranking resets then
- Email your full list at 8am PST with a direct link; never say "upvote," say "check it out"
- Drop into relevant Slack and Discord communities at 9am
- Post to Twitter/LinkedIn at 9am and 3pm
- Reply to every comment within 30 minutes — comment velocity is a ranking signal
- Stagger team members sharing in their personal networks through the day

**The maker comment** is your one shot to be human in front of 50,000 people — make it personal and story-driven, not a feature list.

## LinkedIn Organic Growth (Founders & Execs)

**Phase 1 — Consistency (months 1–2):**
- Post 4x/week minimum, reply to every comment within 2 hours
- Comment genuinely on 10 posts a day
- Engage 20 people in your target audience a day

**Phase 2 — Voice (months 2–4):**
- Find the three topics you have the strongest opinions on
- One clearly-stated "hot take" post a week
- Share real results, including uncomfortable ones

**Phase 3 — Amplification (month 4+):**
- Repurpose your best phase-2 posts as carousels, video, and text
- Collaborate — tag people, joint posts, interviews
- Use polls to surface engagement and audience signal

**Benchmarks:**
- Months 1–3: 0–500 followers, building the foundation
- Months 3–6: 500–2,000, gaining momentum
- Months 6–12: 2,000–10,000, if consistent

## High-ROI Growth Patterns

Tactics that have worked for a decade still work — just adjust for scale and cost. Don't overcomplicate it.

1. **"Powered by" footer** — a zero-cost viral loop, if your output is visible to others (Typeform, Hotjar, Canva)
2. **Reverse trial** — full product for 14 days beats a limited free tier every time
3. **Win competitors' unhappy customers** — search G2/Trustpilot for 2–3 star reviews; these people are in-market and frustrated
4. **Co-marketing with adjacent products** — same ICP, different function. Joint webinar, shared list, email swap
5. **Guest post on a high-authority site** — one HubSpot or TechCrunch article beats 50 posts on your own blog
6. **Build a free single-purpose tool** — it compounds into backlinks, signups, and brand awareness (CoSchedule's headline analyzer, HubSpot's website grader)

## Metrics That Matter, by Stage

| Stage | North Star | Leading indicators |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-PMF | Weekly active users returning | Retention, NPS |
| Early growth | MoM revenue growth | CAC, payback period |
| Scale | Net revenue retention | Expansion MRR, churn |
| Mature | Market share | LTV/CAC ratio |

**Ignore at early stage:** total signups without activation, page views, social followers, downloads without DAU behind them.
